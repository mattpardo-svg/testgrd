Upload rao_transfer_A.zip.part1 and rao_transfer_A.zip.part2 (20 MB and 9 MB; GitHub limit 25 MB per file).
On the other computer, put both parts in one folder and join them in PowerShell:

    cmd /c copy /b rao_transfer_A.zip.part1+rao_transfer_A.zip.part2 rao_transfer_A.zip

Check the result (must equal the line in rao_transfer_A.sha256.txt):

    Get-FileHash rao_transfer_A.zip -Algorithm SHA256

Then unzip rao_transfer_A.zip into an empty folder and follow TRANSFER.md inside.
